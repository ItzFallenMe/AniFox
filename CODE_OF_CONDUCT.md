# Code of conduct

AniFox is a project for people who like building and watching things together.
Participation should be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of
experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about code and kind about people. Critique the change, not the
  contributor.
- Assume good intent, ask questions before assigning motives, and accept that
  maintainers may decline a change that does not fit the project.
- Keep discussion on topic and free of piracy links, credentials or personal
  data. AniFox is a front-end; do not post copyrighted media or keys.
- Respect requests to stop a conversation or move it to a private channel.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats, sexualised attention or unwelcome comments.
- Discriminatory jokes or imagery, including in issues, code and commit
  messages.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Spam, repeated off-topic promotion, or deliberately derailing threads.
- Posting secrets, private keys or personal data belonging to others.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, reject contributions or
temporarily or permanently ban accounts for behaviour that breaks this policy.
Decisions are made in good faith and aim to keep the project usable for
everyone. To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through the
profile linked in the README; reports are handled confidentially.

This policy applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and
any space where someone represents the project.
